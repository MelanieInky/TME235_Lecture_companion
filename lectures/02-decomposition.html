Deviatoric / Spherical Decomposition

Deviatoric and Volumetric decomposition of a tensor

Consider either the stress or the strain tensor. They can be decomposed into its spherical and volumetric part (Lecture notes 1.15 and 1.16).

\[ \sigma_{ij} = \sigma_{ij}^{\text{sph}} + \sigma_{ij}^{\text{dev}} \]

where \(\sigma_{ij}^{\text{sph}} = \frac{1}{3} \sigma_{kk} \delta_{ij}\) is called the spherical part of the tensor. The deviatoric part is then given by

\[ \sigma_{ij}^{\text{dev}} = \sigma_{ij} - \sigma_{ij}^{\text{sph}} = \sigma_{ij} - \frac{1}{3} \sigma_{kk} \delta_{ij} \]

In matrix notation:

\[ \boldsymbol{\sigma} = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_{11} & \sigma_{12} & \sigma_{13} \\ \sigma_{21} & \sigma_{22} & \sigma_{23} \\ \sigma_{31} & \sigma_{32} & \sigma_{33} \end{pmatrix} \]

\[ \sigma_m = \frac{1}{3}\,\text{tr}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}) = \frac{1}{3}\left(\sigma_{11}+\sigma_{22}+\sigma_{33}\right) \]

\[ \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{sph} = \sigma_m \, \mathbf{I} = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_m & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \sigma_m & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \sigma_m \end{pmatrix} \]

\[ \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{dev} = \boldsymbol{\sigma} - \sigma_m \, \mathbf{I} = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_{11}-\sigma_m & \sigma_{12} & \sigma_{13} \\ \sigma_{21} & \sigma_{22}-\sigma_m & \sigma_{23} \\ \sigma_{31} & \sigma_{32} & \sigma_{33}-\sigma_m \end{pmatrix} \]
